Kol Korei · A call from the heart

Fortunate is the one who takes part in this mitzvah

We turn to you from the depths of our hearts on behalf of Mosdot Peer Hatalmud in Jerusalem. Our aim is to educate Jewish boys who thirst for the word of Hashem and long for Torah, yirat shamayim and good middot. Reaching them takes a warm heart that understands what they lack and fills that gap with feeling, raising them step by step.

For this we chose a devoted, warm staff who can reach the depths of their hearts and lift their spirits with gentle hands. Thank G-d, many knock on our doors, and many boys now breathe freely in an atmosphere of Torah, “like cold water to a weary soul.”

But im ein kemach, ein Torah. Caring for our boys in body and soul, with three hot meals every day, carries great costs.

Now we ask you to help us build a permanent home for these boys: a beit midrash, a dining room and rooms where they can live and grow, under one roof. May Hashem fulfil all your heart's wishes for good.

Rabbi Yosef DarshanRosh HaYeshiva

A lifetime of dedication to teaching Torah. HaRav Yosef Darshan has devoted his entire life to raising boys in Torah, alongside the Gedolei Yisrael of the generation, among them Maran HaRav Ovadia Yosef zt”l.

Founded 5760 (2000) · Jerusalem

Where every boy finds his place in Torah

Peer Hatalmud takes in boys who left other schools for personal, family or emotional reasons. A devoted staff gives each one structure with the warmth of a father, a personal mentor, three hot meals a day and a place to sleep, until he can stand on his own. Every boy who completed the three-year program went on to a mainstream yeshiva, and today the great yeshivot ask for Peer Hatalmud students.

80talmidim, ages 14 to 17, learning all day
35married men in the full-day kollel
65men in the evening kollel
25men in the Friday kollel
104families in need supported by our chesed organization

The Rosh Yeshiva surely remembers how I looked at my entrance exam, my heart broken from my previous schools. Three years passed at the speed of light, and the sun rose for me. The first place I ever felt someone shared my pain and stood by me in my hardest hours was here. Now I stand on my own feet.

G.B.Ch., a graduate, in a letter to the Rosh Yeshiva
The new building · Ramat Shlomo, Jerusalem

Everything under one roof

The new building brings the whole yeshiva together: learning, meals and a place to live, in one home of Jerusalem stone.

120seats in the Beit Midrash

The Beit Midrash

The heart of the yeshiva: a bright hall with room for 120 to learn all day, a carved wooden Aron Kodesh and sefarim lining the walls.

  • 120 seats and shtenders
  • Aron Kodesh and bimah
  • A library of sefarim all around
$540,000to dedicate the Beit Midrash

The dining hall

Three hot meals every day, for boys whose physical needs must never stand in the way of their growth. A warm room where the yeshiva sits together as a family.

  • Seating for the whole yeshiva
  • A full kitchen
  • Three serving stations
$360,000to dedicate the dining hall

A room of his own

For many of our boys, the yeshiva is the only stable home they have. Each of the 30 new rooms gives four boys a calm and warm place to live.

  • Four beds, each with a reading lamp
  • Wooden closets and a shelf of sefarim
  • A desk for learning
  • A private shower and bathroom
