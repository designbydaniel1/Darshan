Background

A lifesaver for boys who fell through the cracks

Peer Hatalmud was founded to care for young men who, for personal, family or emotional reasons, need specialized, warm and dedicated attention, and to keep them from the negative influences of the street that can lead them away from Torah observance.

Our students came from other schools where they couldn't adjust to a cold, stern atmosphere. Here, a faculty of dedicated teachers keeps a warm, fatherly relationship with every boy, building the trust, self-confidence and pride he needs to succeed.

Our success

Proof that warmth works

200+students nurtured over the years
50boys learning with us today
100%of graduates continued to mainstream yeshivot

Every boy who completed our three-year curriculum went on to a mainstream yeshiva and an unparalleled road of success. Many have since built families of their own in Israel.

How we help

More than a classroom

Building character

A creative, warm and uplifting home that shapes each boy's personality and gives him confidence for life.

Personal mentors

A high teacher-to-student ratio and personal mentors for every boy who needs special attention.

Meals & lodging

Three hot meals a day and a place to sleep, so no boy's physical needs stand in the way of his growth.

Facing adolescence

Spiritual guides who act as mentors and, when needed, a stand-in for parents.

Learning beyond the day

Shiurim for new olim and children, a Friday and Shabbat kollel, an evening kollel, and a chesed organization.

Lasting independence

We stabilize each student's soul so he can stand on his own for the rest of his life.

Leadership

Rabbi Yosef Darshan, Rosh Yeshiva

Rabbi Darshan brings many years of experience with boys like ours. He is known for his extreme dedication and love for his students and for the great lengths he goes to for their success, all of which comes naturally to him out of love for his students, community and congregation.
